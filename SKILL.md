# Trustx Hub and Viable Group — Brand Guidelines

AI automation business. Aesthetic direction: **minimal & premium** — refined,
spacious, confident. Think Linear / Vercel / Stripe.

## Audience
We sell to small businesses & solopreneurs, e-commerce brands, agencies &
consultants, real estate, and mid-size to enterprise companies. Always speak to
outcomes — time saved, leads captured, costs cut — never to AI jargon.

## Colors
Use a restrained palette. ONE accent color, never more.
- Base dark:   `#0A0A0A`  (near-black, for text and dark sections)
- Base light:  `#FAFAFA`  (off-white background)
- Muted:       `#6B7280`  (secondary text, captions)
- Border:      `#E5E7EB`  (1px subtle borders)
- Accent:      `#4F46E5`  (deep indigo — CTAs, links, highlights only)
- Accent dark: `#4338CA`  (hover/pressed states)

> Swap the accent hex if the real brand color differs. Keep it to one accent.

## Typography
- Headings: **Inter** (or Geist) — large scale, tight tracking, bold weight.
- Body:     **Inter** — regular weight, generous line-height (1.6+).
- Strong type hierarchy: oversized hero headline, clear step-down to subheads.

## Layout & Feel
- Generous whitespace. Let sections breathe.
- Soft shadows, 1px subtle borders (`#E5E7EB`), `rounded-2xl` cards.
- Subtle motion only: gentle fade/slide-in on scroll, smooth hover states.
  Nothing flashy.
- Fully responsive, mobile-first.

## Voice & Copy
- Confident, benefit-led, concise. No buzzwords, no "leverage synergies."
- Lead with the result, then the how. ("Stop losing leads overnight — our
  automations reply, qualify, and book while you sleep.")
- CTAs are direct verbs: "Book a Call", "Get Started", "See It Work".

## Contact / CTAs
- Primary CTA: Book a call/demo.
- Email: trustxhub@gmail.com
- [Add booking link — Calendly etc. — when available]

## Accessibility
- Semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly, alt text on all images.
- Maintain strong contrast (accent on white passes AA for large text/buttons).
